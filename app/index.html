FAQ.

Can anyone contribute?

Yes. Use an account you’re authorized to inspect, follow the contribution skill and open a pull request.

Do I need to share my bank data?

No. Keep credentials and raw records local. Public pull requests contain code, synthetic fixtures and privacy-safe reports.

How do bounties work?

Each target lists a $150 or $200 bounty. See the round index and its issue for terms.

Does an integration prove payment?

It interprets supplied data. Bank-origin authentication and signed attestations require a separate system.

Is OpenPlaid part of Plaid or a bank?

No. OpenPlaid is an independent open-source project, unaffiliated with Plaid or the named financial institutions.
