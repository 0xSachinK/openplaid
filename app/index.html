Is OpenPlaid a bank or a Plaid product?

No. OpenPlaid is an independent open-source project. It is not affiliated with Plaid Inc., Mercury, or other named financial institutions.

Do I have to share my banking data?

No. Bank sessions and raw evidence stay local. Public contributions contain code, synthetic or carefully sanitized fixtures, and minimal test reports. This website has no bank-data upload.

Does an adapter produce a payment proof?

It interprets supplied data. Authenticating its bank origin and generating a signed payment attestation require a separate system. Never release funds based on a parser result alone.

Can anyone contribute?

Yes. Use your own authorized account and coding agent, follow the contribution skill, and submit a pull request. Testing, failure reports, documentation and maintenance all help.
