# Integration icons

The integration grid uses each institution's public brand icon for identification. The marks belong to their respective owners and are not licensed under this repository's MIT license. Displaying a mark does not imply an affiliation or an endorsed integration.

Mercury's icon comes from [Mercury's website](https://mercury.com/icon.svg). Most bounty icons are copies of public website favicons retrieved through Google's favicon cache or the institutions' own sites. The Easypaisa and Access Bank marks come from [Wikimedia Commons](https://commons.wikimedia.org/wiki/File:Easypaisa_Digital_Bank_logo.png) and [Wikimedia Commons](https://commons.wikimedia.org/wiki/File:Access_Bank_Logo.png). The Banco Unión mark comes from [its website](https://bancounion.com.bo/). Icons are stored locally so visitors do not load tracking services just to see the grid.

Please open an issue or pull request if an icon is wrong or out of date.
